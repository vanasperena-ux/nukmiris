NukMiris Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

NukMiris is an appointment management service currently in development. It is designed to help small businesses manage appointments and communicate with customers through WhatsApp.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how NukMiris is used, information processed by the service may include:

  • Customer names and telephone numbers.
  • Appointment dates, times, and service details.
  • WhatsApp messages related to appointment management.
  • Appointment confirmations, cancellations, and changes.
  • Business account and scheduling preferences.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments.
  • Send confirmations and appointment reminders.
  • Process rescheduling and cancellation requests.
  • Synchronize appointments with connected calendars.
  • Maintain service reliability and security.
  • Respond to support and privacy inquiries.

3. WhatsApp Integration

NukMiris is designed to use the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta, for appointment-related communication. Relevant messages and contact details may be transmitted through Meta's services.

4. Google Calendar Integration

Where a business authorizes Google Calendar integration, NukMiris may access calendar availability and create, update, or cancel appointment events as needed to provide scheduling functionality.

Google Calendar information is intended to be used for appointment management and related service operations.

5. Service Providers and Data Sharing

NukMiris may rely on third-party technology providers, including Meta and Google, to deliver its functionality. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the security of the service.

NukMiris does not intend to sell customer personal information.

6. Information Security and Retention

NukMiris aims to apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information it processes.

Information will be retained according to operational requirements, applicable legal obligations, and documented retention practices as the service develops.

7. Privacy Rights and Data Deletion

Individuals may contact NukMiris to request information about personal data associated with them, request corrections, or request deletion where applicable. Requests will be assessed under applicable law and relevant service obligations.

Send privacy or deletion requests to: vanasperena@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as NukMiris develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised update date.

9. Contact
